# Mooncake Fundraising and Community Service

## Overview

A class-led community initiative organized during Grade 11. Our class sold mooncakes to raise funds for a charitable activity and later participated in a visit to deliver donations.

The initiative was organized by students, combining fundraising with community service and an opportunity to strengthen relationships among classmates.

## My Contributions

- Participated in the mooncake fundraising activity.
- Took part in the charitable visit and donation delivery.
- Collaborated with classmates throughout the initiative.
